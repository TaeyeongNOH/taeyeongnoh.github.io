About

Hi, I’m Taeyeong. I’m a researcher at CryptoLab Inc. in Seoul, working on fully homomorphic encryption. Most of my work focuses on the CKKS scheme: making it faster and making its security analysis rigorous.

I’m interested in the guarantees that real systems rely on. Some are precisely stated but remain unproved, supported by heuristics and experiments. Others have yet to be defined. I want to define these guarantees and prove them. I started with FHE, and I hope to bring the same approach to other systems.

Before joining CryptoLab, I studied mathematics and computer science at Seoul National University, where my main interests were analysis and probability theory.
